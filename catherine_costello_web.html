CATHERINE COSTELLO

Senior Investigator

Catherine Costello is a retired Detective Chief Inspector with the Metropolitan Police Service, London, with over 25 years' operational experience in criminal investigations.

She specialised in Missing Persons, Child Protection and Domestic Violence cases and brings extensive investigative knowledge and expertise to Searching For The Missing.

Catherine is highly respected for her tenacity, discretion and compassionate approach when assisting families and private clients.
