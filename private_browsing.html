Private Browsing

OpenBrowse will not save information about this browsing session.

You are now browsing privately

While this window is open, OpenBrowse will not keep a record of the pages you visit or the searches you make.

  • Browsing history will not be saved.
  • Search history will not be saved.
  • Temporary cookies will be removed when this window closes.
  • Downloaded files and saved bookmarks will remain on the computer.
Please note: Private Browsing does not make you anonymous on the Internet. Websites, network administrators and Internet service providers may still be able to record your activity.

OpenBrowse Private Browsing does not protect you from:

  • Monitoring by your employer or school.
  • Tracking by websites and advertisers.
  • Internet service provider records.
  • Downloaded files remaining on your computer.
  • Bookmarks saved during this session.
  • Malicious software or viruses.
